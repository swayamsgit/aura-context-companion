People you know

Save a name, a relationship, a photo and one useful detail. You choose who you are with. Aura never tries to recognise a face.

≡

Everyday moments

Write down what happened, by typing or dictating. Keep a simple routine for the day and tick things off.

↗

A check-in you control

Pick the notes to include, review the draft, then copy it or open it in WhatsApp or email. Aura sends nothing itself.

Made to be gentle

  • Large text and high contrast settings
  • Read any card aloud
  • Works offline once opened, and installs to your home screen
  • Backup and restore with one file

Clear about its limits

  • Not a medical device. No medication, diagnosis or safety advice
  • Notes with health or medication words are blocked
  • No camera, microphone recording, face recognition or tracking
  • Notes are stored unencrypted in this browser. Keep them everyday and low-sensitivity
